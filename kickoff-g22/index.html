Definition

A curriculum gap is content the MD program is expected to teach, by accreditation (LCME), our own program objectives (MEPOs), an EEC decision, law, the licensing and subject exams (USMLE, NBME), the AAMC Core EPAs or the national norm (AAMC SCOPE), that we teach too little or not at all, do not assess, or that our students do not experience or do not master.

It is not a topic beyond what the standards expect (that is a G3.1 emerging topic), and not content that is taught but untagged on the map (a mapping issue). The definition is in the charter, Appendix A.

Terms Used Today

G2.2 · Curriculum Gap Working Group
This group: gives every gap in content the program is expected to teach a documented decision.
G3.1 · Emerging Topics Working Group
The sister group, same co-leads: judges topics beyond what the program is expected to teach.
Gap register
The list of known curriculum gaps, one row each, with evidence, tier, USMLE tag, route, owner and decision. It is on the dashboard's G2.2 page and in Appendix D of the charter.
Tier 1, Tier 2, Monitor
Priority tiers. Tier 1: named in the 2026–27 LCME Standard 7, plus nutrition. Tier 2: named in the 2027–28 Standard 7. Monitor: every other gap.
Address, Defer, Monitor
The three decisions the EEC can make on a gap (charter Appendix E).
Mapping issue
The curriculum map shows a gap, but other evidence shows the content is taught or experienced; the fix is a map correction.
EEC
The Executive Education Committee, which decides on curriculum recommendations.
PCCS, CCS, CIS
The Pre-Clerkship Curriculum Subcommittee (Phase 1), the Clinical Curriculum Subcommittee (Phases 2 and 3), and the Curricular Integration Subcommittee (AOCs and threads).
Clinical Competency group
The Director of Clinical Competency, the POM directors and the Clinical Competency Mentors; reviews clinical-skills gaps.
LCME Standard 7
The accreditation standard for MD curriculum content.
MEPOs
Our Medical Education Program Objectives.
RCE
A required clinical experience that students log in Phase 2.
USMLE tag
Whether a gap's content is on Step 1, Step 2 CK, both or neither; it decides whether PCCS or CCS leads.
AAMC SCOPE
An annual AAMC survey of medical school curricula, reporting the percent of schools that require each topic.
GQ and Y2Q
AAMC's Graduation Questionnaire and Year 2 Questionnaire, in which students rate their preparation by topic.
