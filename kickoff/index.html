Working definition

An emerging healthcare topic is knowledge or a capability that is changing, or likely to change, how our graduates practice in the next five to ten years, and that the MD program is not yet expected to teach by accreditation (LCME), our own program objectives (MEPOs), the AAMC Core EPAs, the licensing and subject exams (USMLE, NBME), or the national norm (AAMC SCOPE).

It is not a topic we are already expected to teach, even if we teach it too little or not at all (that is a G2.2 gap), and not a topic chosen only because someone champions it. This is a draft: the group confirms or changes the wording today.

⚖II. Gap or Emerging, and Where the Evidence Comes From3:07–3:12
Slide 8 of 31

If accreditation (LCME), our own program objectives (MEPOs), the AAMC Core EPAs, the USMLE or NBME exams, or the national norm (AAMC SCOPE: what most schools require) already expects a topic, it is a curriculum gap and goes to G2.2, whether or not we teach it now. Only what lies beyond those expectations is an emerging topic for this group.

Slide 9 of 31

Some sources require a topic (accreditation, our MEPOs, EEC decisions, law); others set a strong expectation (the USMLE and NBME exams, the Core EPAs, residency readiness, the SCOPE norm). Any of them makes a topic expected: a G2.2 gap if we do not teach it. The next six slides give the strengths and limits of each.

Terms Used Today

G2.2 · Curriculum Gap Working Group
The strategic plan initiative that closes gaps in content the program is already expected to teach.
G3.1 · Emerging Topics Working Group
This group: builds the framework for judging topics beyond what the program is expected to teach.
Evaluative framework
The criteria, evidence, topic intake form and scoring and decision rules this group builds and pilots in Year 1.
EEC
The Executive Education Committee, which decides on curriculum recommendations.
LCME Standard 7
The accreditation standard for MD curriculum content.
USMLE
The United States Medical Licensing Examination: Step 1 and Step 2 CK test what is in their published content outlines.
AAMC SCOPE
An annual AAMC survey of medical school curricula, reporting the percent of schools that require each topic.
GQ and Y2Q
AAMC's Graduation Questionnaire and Year 2 Questionnaire, in which students rate their preparation by topic.
Draft call
The starting gap or emerging label on a listed topic, for the group to confirm or change.
